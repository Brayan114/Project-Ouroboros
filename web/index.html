💡 Three Core Innovations
  • Hardware Indirection Cache (HIT): Sub-nanosecond entry lookup with Direct Payload Embedding (≤ 16B).
  • Real-Time Entropy Predictor: Bypasses high-entropy streams (≥ 5.4b) to eliminate size expansion.
  • Near-Memory PIM Dispatch: Offloads data-parallel math directly into DRAM controllers.
